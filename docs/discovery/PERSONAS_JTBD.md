## ORG — The organizer of a recurring informal game

- **Who:** a 22–45-year-old who runs "Mittwochskick" or a Sunday basketball run for 10–30 regulars. Often the person who once booked the hall or found the court. (Archetype from forum, Meetup and organizer sources — MODERATE.)
- **Situation:** every week, 2–3 days before the game.
- **Problem:** has to ask who's coming, chase maybes, decide whether to cancel, find replacements, and front the hall cost when people drop out.
- **Current workaround:** WhatsApp group + poll or Events; PayPal Friends & Family; sometimes Spond, SpielerPlus or JUGAD.
- **JTBD:** *When my weekly game is coming up, I want to know early and reliably who is playing and fill the empty spots, so that the game happens with even teams without me chasing people.*
- **Pain points:** chasing RSVPs (P-03); last-minute dropouts (P-02); paying for no-shows (P-06); group slowly shrinking (P-09); phone numbers exposed to strangers.
- **Desired outcome:** a game that "runs itself" each week: enough players by the deadline, a clear go/no-go, everyone reminded.
- **Evidence:** MODERATE (Meetup rules, platform policies, JUGAD reviews, vendor blogs). German-specific voice: WEAK.

## DROP — The drop-in player without a group

- **Who:** moved to a new city, an expat or student, or friends stopped playing. Wants to play 1×/week at a fitting level. (Expat threads, Berlin fragmentation — MODERATE.)
- **Situation:** free evening or weekend; or a new phase of life (moved, new job).
- **Problem:** doesn't know where games are; joining strangers feels risky; games get cancelled.
- **Current workaround:** Facebook and Meetup groups, CeleBreak (paid, 3 cities), JUGAD, showing up at a court.
- **JTBD:** *When I have time to play this week, I want to find a nearby game at my level that will actually happen, so that I can just show up and play.*
- **Pain points:** fragmented discovery (P-04); unclear level and atmosphere (P-07/08); cancelled games (P-02).
- **Desired outcome:** becoming a regular in one good game, not browsing forever.
- **Evidence:** MODERATE.

## UND — Underserved player (women/FLINTA*, beginners, 35+)

- **Who:** wants to play but finds open games male-dominated, too aggressive or too strong.
- **Situation:** same as DROP, plus an uncertain welcome.
- **Problem:** mixed open games feel unsafe or intimidating; dedicated sessions are oversubscribed (Berlin FLINTA* basketball, taz 2026).
- **Current workaround:** dedicated groups, Walking Football, not playing.
- **JTBD:** *When I want to play, I want to know a game is explicitly for people like me, so that I can join without feeling out of place.*
- **Pain points:** P-07, P-08.
- **Desired outcome:** a recurring, explicitly welcoming game.
- **Evidence:** MODERATE–STRONG for women in Berlin basketball; MODERATE for 50+ (Footy Addicts reviews); WEAK for beginners in Germany.

## Deferred: Venue operator (commercial soccer hall)

- **Hypothesis only.** Operators already use webshops, Playtomic, USC or B2B tools (anny). A possible job is filling off-peak courts. No evidence was collected from operators. Validate with 3–5 operator interviews before any build (A-07).

## JTBD hierarchy

1. **Core job (ORG):** *Make my regular game happen reliably every week.*
2. **Core job (DROP/UND):** *Find a game I fit into and become a regular.*
3. Supporting jobs: find a place that's allowed and suitable; split the cost fairly; feel safe with strangers.

Both core jobs meet in one object: **a recurring game with open spots**. The organizer needs players, and the drop-in player needs a reliable game. That object is the centre of the product ([FINAL_PRODUCT_SPEC.md](FINAL_PRODUCT_SPEC.md)).
